# TCL Phase 1 Protocol Addendum: Kaggle Runs

Status: locked before any Kaggle Phase 1 data is generated. Written October 16, 2026.

This addendum covers the GPU part of Phase 1. It also responds to three defects I found in the TCL-v0 records after the local Phase 1 gate. The local H1/H2 results in `docs/phase1/TCL-Phase1-Local-Checkpoint.md` stand as recorded. The runs below are a **separately reported replication on corrected data**, not a replacement for those results.

## 1. Defects Found in the TCL-v0 Records

| Defect | Evidence | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Raw confidence averaged over non-answer tokens.** Generation did not stop at the answer, and confidence was the geometric mean over every generated token. | Gemma outputs run on to the 12-token limit, e.g. `Denver Broncos.\n\nThe context describes the team that...`. 301 of 1,000 Gemma SQuAD outputs contain a newline. | Gemma's raw confidence partly measures the fluency of its explanation, not its answer. This can explain its near-chance raw AUROC on SQuAD (0.534), and so part of the probe's advantage there. |
| **Answer extraction kept the whole output.** `extract_primary_answer` returns the full cleaned text, not the first segment. | `model_answer` equals the full raw output in every Gemma record. | Hidden-state pooling covered explanation tokens, and labels were matched against the full text. |
| **SQuAD splits share passages.** The 1,000-question SQuAD subset is the first 1,000 validation rows, which come from only 2 Wikipedia articles. | 197 of 200 test questions share a passage with training questions. | A probe can learn passage-specific patterns. In-distribution SQuAD results, including Gemma's 0.930 probe AUROC, may be inflated. |

Refusals ("The context does not provide...") are a smaller issue: 146 of Gemma's TriviaQA outputs, all labeled incorrect. Excluding them on the test split leaves the Gemma probe advantage largely intact (0.799 vs 0.705 raw AUROC), so they do not explain it.

## 2. Data

| Dataset | Source | Questions | Train / validation / test | Split unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TriviaQA | `mandarjoshi/trivia_qa`, `rc.nocontext`, validation, first 4,500 rows | 4,500 | 2,500 / 500 / 1,500 | Question (random, seed 42) |
| SQuAD v1.1 | `rajpurkar/squad`, validation | 4,500 sampled | 2,500 / 500 / 1,500 | **Wikipedia article**: no article appears in two splits |
| NQ-Open | `google-research-datasets/nq_open`, validation (3,610 rows) | 3,610 | 1,610 / 500 / 1,500 | Question (random, seed 42) |

For SQuAD, articles are shuffled (seed 42) and assigned whole to test, then validation, then train, until each split reaches its target. Each split is then randomly subsampled to its exact size.

The split files are generated by `tcl_experiments/scripts/prepare_phase1_kaggle_data.py` and committed before any model is run.

**Models:** Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, Phi-3.5-mini-instruct, Gemma 2B-it. That gives 9 model-dataset pairings.

**Size rule (program plan, Section 6).** A pairing is retained only if its test split has at least 150 correct and 150 incorrect answers. This is checked from labels alone, before any metric is computed. Dropped pairings are recorded.

## 3. Generation and Labels (Inference v2)

Script: `tcl_experiments/scripts/run_inference_v2.py`.

- **Prompts:** the same chat prompts as TCL-v0.
- **Stopping:** generation stops at the tokenizer's EOS token or the model's end-of-turn token. `max_new_tokens = 32`.
- **Answer span:** the generated tokens before the first newline. The answer text is that span, stripped.
- **Labels:** the existing `strict_answer_segment_match_v2` rules applied to the answer span. Recorded as `strict_answer_segment_match_v2_answer_span`.
- **Refusal flag:** a regex marks outputs that decline to answer. Refusals are labeled incorrect in the primary analysis. A secondary analysis excludes them.
- **No manual review** in this round. Labels are automatic on all splits. This avoids the score-dependent review selection noted in the local checkpoint. I will audit a random sample of 100 test rows per retained pairing and report the label error rate.

## 4. Signals Recorded

All single-pass signals are recorded for every question. Semantic entropy is recorded for the test split only.

| Signal | Definition |
|---|---|
| Raw (answer span) | Geometric-mean token probability over answer-span tokens. **This replaces TCL-v0's raw confidence as the raw baseline.** |
| Raw (full output) | TCL-v0's original method, over all generated tokens. Kept to measure the effect of the fix. |
| Hidden states | Layers at 25%, 50%, and 100% depth; pooling `answer_mean` and `answer_last` over the answer span. Stored as float16 arrays. |
| P(True) | Zero-shot self-evaluation: the model is shown the question (and context, for SQuAD) and its own answer, and asked whether the answer is correct. Score = P(True) / (P(True) + P(False)) from the next-token distribution. |
| Semantic entropy | 10 samples at temperature 1.0, answer spans clustered by bidirectional entailment with `microsoft/deberta-large-mnli`, discrete semantic entropy over clusters (Farquhar et al., 2024). Confidence = -entropy. |
| Self-consistency label | Fraction of the 10 samples in the same meaning cluster as the greedy answer. Stored for Phase 3; not used in Phase 1 decisions. |

## 5. Analyses and Decision Rules

Fitting, model selection, metrics, and bootstrap are exactly as in `docs/phase1/TCL-Phase1-Protocol.md` (unweighted fits, C chosen by 5-fold CV on train, Platt map on validation, 1,000 paired bootstrap resamples, seeds 0-2 with seed 0 primary). The probe uses final-layer `answer_mean` features, matching TCL-v0.

### H1-R (replication of H1 on corrected data)

For each retained pairing: `AUROC(probe) - AUROC(calibrated raw, answer span)` with a 95% bootstrap CI.

**H1-R passes** if the CI lower bound is above zero in **at least two thirds of retained pairings** (rounded up). This is the same proportion as H1's 4 of 6.

### H2-R (replication of H2 on corrected data)

For each model, probes are transferred between every ordered pair of retained datasets for that model. Retention is defined as in `docs/phase1/TCL-Phase1-Protocol.md`.

**H2-R passes** if retention is at least 0.70 in **at least two thirds of transfer directions** (rounded up).

### Baseline comparisons (reported, not gated)

For each retained pairing, with bootstrap CIs on the differences:

- probe vs P(True): AUROC, AURC, ECE, Brier (P(True) receives the same validation Platt map as every other method)
- probe vs semantic entropy: AUROC and AURC only, because semantic entropy has no validation-split values to calibrate on
- raw (answer span) vs raw (full output): AUROC, to measure the effect of the confidence fix

### Layer sweep (descriptive, Qwen only)

On Qwen's three datasets: validation AUROC for each layer (25%, 50%, 100%) and pooling (`answer_mean`, `answer_last`). The best validation configuration is carried into Phase 2 as the trust head's input layer. Test results for the sweep are reported but not used for selection.

## 6. How Results Will Be Read

- H1-R and H2-R are reported **next to** the original H1/H2 failures, never instead of them.
- If H1-R passes but H1 failed, the conclusion is that the original failure was at least partly caused by the defects in Section 1, and the corrected result is the one Phase 2 builds on.
- If H1-R also fails, the case for TCL rests on H3, as already recorded.

## 7. Amendments

Each amendment below was made after inspecting labels and output text only, before any metric was computed on the Kaggle records.

**2026-10-17: refusal detection on the answer span.** Inference v2 recorded the refusal flag on the full generated output. For Gemma, this also matched disclaimers added after a real answer, e.g. `Acura.\n\nThe context does not specify...` (25 TriviaQA and 85 SQuAD outputs, some of them correct answers). The analysis now detects refusals with the same pattern on the answer span; the full-output flag is kept as `refusal_full_output`. This affects only the secondary "refusals excluded" analysis. With the corrected flag, Gemma refuses 1,339 of 4,500 TriviaQA questions and 774 of 4,500 SQuAD questions. Because refusals are labeled incorrect in the primary analysis, a probe that recognizes refusals gains AUROC without judging answers; the secondary analysis is therefore essential for reading Gemma's results.

**2026-10-17: Qwen NQ-Open dropped.** Its test split has 101 correct answers, below the 150 required by the size rule.

**2026-10-18: Gemma NQ-Open dropped.** Its test split has 107 correct answers. Gemma refuses 1,782 of 3,610 NQ-Open questions (answer-span detection).

**2026-10-18: Phi SQuAD dropped.** Its test split has 114 incorrect answers (Phi answers 92.4% of SQuAD test questions correctly). Retained pairings: Qwen TriviaQA, Qwen SQuAD, Gemma TriviaQA, Gemma SQuAD, Phi TriviaQA, Phi NQ-Open. H1-R therefore needs at least 4 of 6 pairings, and H2-R at least 4 of its 6 transfer directions.
