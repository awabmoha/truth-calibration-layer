# Code of Conduct

This is a small, single-author research repository. Anyone who opens an issue, a pull request, or a discussion here is expected to keep it respectful and focused on the work.

## Expected

- Discuss the research, the code, and the data, not the people.
- Criticism of methods and results is welcome, including of negative results. Be specific: point to the file, the number, or the step you disagree with.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure.

## Not Acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Spam or repeated off-topic posts.

## Enforcement

I moderate this repository. I may edit, hide, or delete comments, issues, and pull requests that break this code, and block accounts that do so repeatedly. To report a problem privately, contact me through my GitHub profile ([@awabmoha](https://github.com/awabmoha)) rather than in a public thread.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
